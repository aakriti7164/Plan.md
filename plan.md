# PLAN.md: Two weeks to showcase

## The ONE improvement
Show a measured before/after. Users take a gamified Baseline Challenge, play
one case, then take the identical Final Challenge. You present real results
from 15+ strangers.

All five cases stay as-is for range. The measurement loop is built on one.

## Changes, and what's true when each works
| # | Change (owner) | True when done |
|---|----------------|----------------|
| 1 | Deploy the app and log sessions to one store (tech teammate, by day 2). | You open the store and see your own test session within a minute. |
| 2 | Write one 4-question scenario outside the five cases, plus a 3-criterion rubric (you). | Two people score the same answers and agree on 80%+. |
| 3 | Send the rubric and the Sources approach to one professor, and ask permission to name them as an advisor (you, by day 3). | You have written feedback and a yes or no on being named. |
| 4 | Add the Baseline Challenge before case 1, with a score reveal (tech teammate). | A new user cannot reach case 1 without submitting answers. |
| 5 | Add the identical Final Challenge after the reflection screen, showing "your before vs. after" plus a one-tap feedback rating (tech teammate). | Finishing 7 decisions routes the user to it automatically. |
| 6 | Add a visible "Sources" expander on every "what actually happened" screen (you, with a teammate on formatting). | Every factual claim in all five reveals links to a citation. |
| 7 | Run 15-20 strangers through the full loop and take stall notes (customer-discovery teammate, days 7-11). | At least 15 completed sessions are in the store. |
| 8 | Build one results slide, one roadmap slide (levels 2-10, events, actions), and a 90-second backup demo video (you, days 12-14). | The demo works offline, and the slide shows real average before/after change. |

## Guardrails
- Day 2: deployed and logging.
- Day 6: baseline and final challenges live.
- Day 11: data collection closed.
- Days 12-14: slides, rehearsal, and backup video only. No new features.

## Deliberately NOT building
- Levels 2-10 on Nepal or any other case (roadmap slide only)
- New cases
- In-person event unlock logic and invitations (roadmap only)
- Petition and call-your-MP actions (one mockup screenshot at most)
- Accounts, profiles, leaderboards, extra gamification
- Redesign of working screens

## Say it honestly
15-20 users is a pilot signal, not proof. Present it as early evidence you're
now testing with advisors, and say plainly that choosing and framing sources
is itself a political act you're building review around.
